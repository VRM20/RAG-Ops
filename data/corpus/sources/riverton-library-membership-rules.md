# Riverton Public Library - Membership and Lending Rules

> Fictional public-sector document created for the RAG-Ops demo corpus. Riverton Public Library is not a real institution. Effective 1 September 2026.

## 1. Membership

Membership is free for residents of Riverton. Non-residents may join for an annual fee of USD 40. Applicants must show a photo ID and proof of address. Children under 14 need a parent or guardian to co-sign the application.

## 2. Borrowing Limits and Loan Periods

Adult members may borrow up to 15 items at a time; children may borrow up to 8 items.

| Item type | Loan period | Renewals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Books and audiobooks | 21 days | Up to 2 renewals |
| Magazines | 7 days | No renewals |
| DVDs and games | 7 days | 1 renewal |

An item cannot be renewed if another member has placed a hold on it.

## 3. Holds

Members may place up to 10 holds. When a held item becomes available, it is kept at the front desk for 7 days before it passes to the next member in the queue.

## 4. Late Returns and Lost Items

Children's items have no late fees. For adult items the late fee is USD 0.25 per item per day, capped at USD 5 per item. A lost or damaged item is charged at its replacement cost plus a USD 10 processing fee. Borrowing is suspended while a member owes more than USD 20.

## 5. Facilities

Meeting rooms can be booked up to 30 days in advance, for a maximum of 3 hours per booking. The second floor is a quiet zone where phone calls are not allowed. Public Wi-Fi sessions last 4 hours and can be renewed at the front desk.
