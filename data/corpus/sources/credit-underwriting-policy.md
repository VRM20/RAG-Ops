# Lumen Ledger Finance - Credit Underwriting Policy

> Fictional document created for the RAG-Ops demo corpus. Classification: Confidential. Owner: Chief Risk Officer. Version 7.0, approved by the Board Risk Committee on 15 February 2026.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the minimum standards for assessing, approving and monitoring credit at Lumen Ledger Finance. Any deviation from this policy must be approved under Section 4.

## 2. Borrower Eligibility

### 2.1 Age and Vintage

The applicant must be at least 21 years old at application and not older than 65 years at loan maturity. For loans against property with an earning co-applicant, the maximum age at maturity is 70 years. MSME borrowers must have a minimum business vintage of 3 years.

### 2.2 Credit Bureau Score

The minimum credit bureau score (CIBIL) is 700. Applicants with scores between 650 and 699 may be approved only with additional collateral cover of 1.5 times the loan amount and a deviation approval. Applicants scoring below 650 are declined.

New-to-credit (NTC) applicants with no bureau history are eligible only for two-wheeler loans up to INR 1 lakh.

## 3. Key Underwriting Ratios

### 3.1 FOIR

The fixed obligation to income ratio (FOIR) must not exceed 55% for salaried applicants and 60% for self-employed applicants.

### 3.2 Loan-to-Value (LTV)

| Product | Maximum LTV |
| --- | --- |
| Loan against residential property | 65% |
| Loan against commercial property | 55% |
| MSME secured business loan | 70% of market value |
| Two-wheeler loan | 85% of on-road price |

### 3.3 DSCR

MSME loans above INR 50 lakh require a minimum debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) of 1.25 times.

## 4. Sanctioning Authority Matrix

| Loan amount | Sanctioning authority |
| --- | --- |
| Up to INR 25 lakh | Branch Credit Manager |
| Above INR 25 lakh up to INR 1 crore | Regional Credit Head |
| Above INR 1 crore up to INR 3 crore | Zonal Credit Committee |
| Above INR 3 crore | Head Office Credit Committee, chaired by the Chief Risk Officer |

Any deviation from this policy must be approved by an authority one level above the standard sanctioning authority for that loan amount.

## 5. Collateral and Valuation

Properties offered as collateral for loans above INR 1 crore require two independent valuations, and the lower of the two values is used for LTV. A legal title search covering at least 30 years is mandatory for all property collateral.

## 6. Field Investigation

Residence verification and business-place verification are mandatory for every loan. A personal discussion (PD) by a credit officer is mandatory for loans above INR 10 lakh.

## 7. Pricing

Interest rates are risk-based. The rate floor is 11.5% per annum. Pricing below the rate floor requires approval from the Chief Risk Officer.

## 8. Negative Profiles

The company does not lend to real-estate developers, cryptocurrency exchanges, arms and ammunition dealers, or any entity on the internal negative list maintained by Credit Risk.

## 9. Early Warning Signals

An account with 2 EMI bounces within 6 months is placed on the watchlist. An account that becomes SMA-1 (31 to 60 days past due) triggers a relationship review by the branch within 7 days.
