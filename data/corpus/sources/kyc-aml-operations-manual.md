# Lumen Ledger Finance - KYC and AML Operations Manual

> Fictional document created for the RAG-Ops demo corpus. Audience: Compliance and Branch Operations. Owner: Head of Compliance (Principal Officer).

## 1. Customer Due Diligence

### 1.1 Identity Documents

Customer identity is established using an officially valid document (OVD): passport, driving licence, voter ID card, Aadhaar, or NREGA job card. When Aadhaar is collected, the first 8 digits must be masked on every stored copy. PAN or Form 60 is mandatory for every borrower.

### 1.2 Video KYC

Video-based customer identification (V-CIP) is permitted. The session must be conducted live by a trained company official, the customer must be geo-tagged within India, and the session recording must be stored securely.

## 2. Customer Risk Categorisation

Every customer is categorised as Low, Medium or High risk at onboarding. Politically exposed persons (PEPs) and customers running cash-intensive businesses onboarded without a face-to-face meeting are always High risk.

Periodic re-KYC is performed every 2 years for High-risk customers, every 8 years for Medium-risk customers and every 10 years for Low-risk customers.

## 3. Enhanced Due Diligence

High-risk customers undergo enhanced due diligence, including verification of the source of funds. Onboarding a PEP requires approval from the Head of Compliance.

## 4. Transaction Monitoring and Reporting

- **Cash Transaction Report (CTR)** - all cash receipts of INR 10 lakh or more in a calendar month from a single customer are reported to FIU-IND by the 15th of the following month.
- **Suspicious Transaction Report (STR)** - filed with FIU-IND within 7 days of the Principal Officer being satisfied that a transaction is suspicious.
- **Counterfeit Currency Report (CCR)** - filed for every counterfeit note detected at a branch.

## 5. Cash Handling at Branches

Branches must not accept cash of INR 2 lakh or more from a single customer in a single day. Any EMI paid in cash above INR 50,000 requires the customer's PAN to be recorded.

## 6. Record Retention

KYC records and transaction records are retained for 5 years after the business relationship ends or the account is closed, whichever is later.

## 7. Tipping-Off Prohibition

Employees must never inform a customer, directly or indirectly, that an STR has been filed or is being considered. Tipping off is a disciplinary offence.

## 8. Training

Annual AML training is mandatory for every employee in Compliance and Branch Operations. The pass mark for the training assessment is 80%.
