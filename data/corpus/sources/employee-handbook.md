# Lumen Ledger Finance - Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

> Fictional document created for the RAG-Ops demo corpus. Applies to all employees of Lumen Ledger Finance Ltd.

## 1. Employment Basics

### 1.1 Probation

Every new employee serves a probation period of 6 months. The reporting manager may extend probation once, by a maximum of 3 months, with written reasons shared with the employee and HR Business Partner.

### 1.2 Notice Period

The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation. Employees in Grade M4 and above serve a notice period of 90 days. Notice period buy-out is allowed only with the approval of the department head.

## 2. Working Hours

Head office (HO) working hours are 9:30 to 18:30, Monday to Friday. Branches work Monday to Saturday, with the second and fourth Saturdays of each month as holidays. Core collaboration hours, during which meetings should be scheduled, are 11:00 to 16:00.

## 3. Hybrid Work

Head office staff may work from home for up to 2 days per week with reporting manager approval. Branch staff and field collection staff are not eligible for work from home. Work-from-home requests must be submitted in the HRMS portal "Lumen People" by Friday of the previous week.

## 4. Leave Policy

### 4.1 Privileged Leave

Employees earn 24 days of privileged leave (PL) per year, accrued at 2 days per month. Unused privileged leave can be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 45 days. At exit, up to 15 days of privileged leave can be encashed at basic salary.

### 4.2 Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

### 4.3 Casual Leave

Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave cannot be combined with privileged leave and cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days.

### 4.4 Parental and Bereavement Leave

Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption. Bereavement leave is 5 working days for the death of an immediate family member.

## 5. Holidays

The company observes 12 holidays per calendar year: 10 fixed holidays published by HR each December and 2 optional holidays that employees choose from the regional list.

## 6. Code of Conduct

### 6.1 Gifts

Employees may accept gifts with a value of up to INR 2,000. Any gift above INR 2,000 must be declared to the Compliance team within 7 days of receipt. Cash gifts of any amount must never be accepted.

### 6.2 Conflicts of Interest

Every employee must submit an annual conflict-of-interest declaration in Lumen People by 30 April. Employees must not process, approve or influence a loan for a relative.

### 6.3 Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)

The company has an Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH framework. Complaints should be filed with the IC within 3 months of the incident. The IC completes its inquiry within 90 days of receiving a complaint.

## 7. Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning allowance of INR 25,000 for approved certifications and courses. Every employee is expected to complete at least 40 hours of training per financial year.

## 8. Separation

Exiting employees complete an exit interview with HR. The full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day, after all company assets are returned.
