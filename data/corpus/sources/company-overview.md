# Lumen Ledger Finance - Company Overview

> Lumen Ledger Finance Ltd. is a fictional company. Every name, number and policy in this corpus was invented for the RAG-Ops demo.

## 1. Who We Are

Lumen Ledger Finance Ltd. is a non-banking finance company (NBFC) headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, founded in 2014. We provide secured MSME business loans, loans against property and two-wheeler loans to customers in tier-2 and tier-3 cities.

As of 31 March 2026 the company operates 212 branches across 9 states, employs 4,860 people and manages assets under management (AUM) of INR 8,450 crore. Gross NPA stood at 2.1% and net NPA at 0.9% for the financial year 2025-26.

## 2. Mission and Values

Our mission is "Credit that compounds trust". The four company values are:

- **Customer First** - explain every fee and term in the customer's preferred language before disbursal.
- **Prudence** - grow only as fast as our risk controls allow.
- **Transparency** - publish our grievance statistics every quarter.
- **Ownership** - every employee is accountable for the customer outcome, not just their task.

## 3. Products

| Product | Ticket size | Tenure | Indicative interest rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MSME secured business loan | INR 5 lakh to INR 2 crore | 3 to 10 years | 14% to 19% p.a. |
| Loan against property (LAP) | INR 10 lakh to INR 5 crore | Up to 15 years | 11.5% to 15% p.a. |
| Two-wheeler loan | INR 40,000 to INR 2.5 lakh | 12 to 48 months | 16% to 24% p.a. |

Processing fees are capped at 2% of the sanctioned amount for all products. There are no foreclosure charges on floating-rate loans to individual borrowers.

## 5. Customer Grievance Redressal

Customers can raise a complaint through the toll-free number 1800-000-0000, by email to care@lumenledger.example, or at any branch. Every complaint is acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 working days.

If a complaint is not resolved within 30 days, the customer may escalate it to the Principal Nodal Officer, who must respond within 10 working days of the escalation.
