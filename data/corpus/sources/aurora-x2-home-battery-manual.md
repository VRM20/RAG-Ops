# Aurora X2 Home Battery - Installation and User Manual

> Fictional product manual created for the RAG-Ops demo corpus. Aurora Energy Systems is not a real company. Manual revision C, March 2026.

## Safety Information

The Aurora X2 must be installed by an Aurora-certified electrician. Never open the battery enclosure: it contains no user-serviceable parts and stores enough energy to cause serious injury. Do not install the unit in a bathroom, in a room used for sleeping, or anywhere it could be exposed to standing water.

In case of fire, do not use water. Leave the building and call the emergency services, then inform them that a lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery is installed.

## Installation Requirements

### Location and Clearances

Mount the unit on a solid concrete or masonry wall that can carry at least four times its weight. Keep a clearance of 30 cm on each side, 60 cm above the unit and 1 m in front of it for ventilation and service access. The bottom of the unit must be at least 15 cm above the floor to protect it from flooding. Avoid direct sunlight: enclosure temperatures above 50 degrees Celsius trigger a thermal shutdown.

### Electrical Connection

Connect the battery to a dedicated 40 A circuit breaker using copper cable with a cross-section of at least 10 mm2. The installer must fit the supplied current transformer on the main grid cable so the system can measure household consumption.

## Commissioning with the Aurora App

1. Install the Aurora Home app and create an account.
2. Press and hold the front button for 5 seconds until the status LED blinks blue, which starts pairing mode.
3. Select "Add device" in the app and confirm the pairing code shown in the app.
4. The battery downloads the latest firmware automatically. An update takes about 15 minutes; do not switch the unit off during an update.

## LED Status Indicators

| LED | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Solid green | Normal operation |
| Blinking green | Charging |
| Solid amber | Standby, or state of charge below 10% |
| Blinking blue | Pairing mode |
| Blinking red | Fault: open the app to see the error code |

## Troubleshooting

| Error code | Cause | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E101 | Over-temperature | Check the clearances and shade the unit; it resumes automatically when the internal temperature falls below 45 degrees Celsius |
| E204 | Grid connection lost | Check the circuit breaker; the unit reconnects automatically within 5 minutes after grid power returns |
| E310 | Cell imbalance | Stop using the unit and contact Aurora support |

## Maintenance

Clean the ventilation grilles with a dry cloth every 6 months. Run the capacity self-test from the app once a year; the result is stored in the warranty record.

## Warranty

The Aurora X2 is covered for 10 years or 6,000 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. At the end of the warranty period the battery is guaranteed to retain at least 70% of its original usable capacity. The product must be registered in the app within 90 days of installation. Installation by a non-certified installer voids the warranty. Warranty claims are handled at support@aurora-energy.example.
