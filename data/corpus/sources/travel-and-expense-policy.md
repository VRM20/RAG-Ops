# Lumen Ledger Finance - Travel and Expense Policy

> Fictional document created for the RAG-Ops demo corpus. Owner: Finance Controller. Effective 1 April 2026.

## 1. Travel Approval

Domestic business travel must be approved by the reporting manager in the "Lumen Spend" portal at least 7 days before the trip, except for documented emergencies. International travel requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer at least 21 days before the trip.

## 2. Air Travel

All grades travel in economy class for flights shorter than 4 hours. Employees in Grade M5 and above may book business class for flights longer than 4 hours. Tickets should be booked at least 14 days in advance where possible.

## 3. Hotel Limits

Maximum hotel tariff per night, excluding taxes:

| City tier | Grades M1 to M3 | Grades M4 and above |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier-1 | INR 7,500 | INR 11,000 |
| Tier-2 | INR 4,500 | INR 7,000 |
| Tier-3 | INR 3,000 | INR 5,000 |

Tier-1 cities are Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune and Kolkata. Grades E1 and E2 follow the M1 to M3 limits.

## 4. Per Diem

The daily allowance for meals and local conveyance is INR 1,500 in Tier-1 cities, INR 1,000 in Tier-2 cities and INR 750 in Tier-3 cities. No per diem is paid for days on which meals are provided by the company or a client.

## 5. Local Travel

Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre and a personal two-wheeler at INR 5 per kilometre. App-based cabs must be booked in the economy category.

## 6. Expense Claims

Claims must be submitted in Lumen Spend within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted more than 60 days after the trip are rejected. Original receipts are mandatory for every expense above INR 500. Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days of approval.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed: alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment, spa services, and laundry for trips shorter than 3 nights.

## 8. Corporate Cards

Corporate credit cards are issued to employees in Grade M3 and above. Personal use of a corporate card is prohibited. Card statements must be reconciled in Lumen Spend by the 5th of every month.
