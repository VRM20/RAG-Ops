# Lumen Ledger Finance - Compensation and Bonus Framework 2026

> Fictional document created for the RAG-Ops demo corpus. Classification: Confidential - HR and Leadership only. Owner: Chief Human Resources Officer.

## 1. Grade Structure and Pay Bands

Annual cost-to-company (CTC) bands, in INR lakh:

| Grade | Minimum | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | 3.0 | 4.5 |
| E2 | 4.5 | 6.5 |
| M1 | 7 | 11 |
| M2 | 11 | 16 |
| M3 | 16 | 24 |
| M4 | 24 | 36 |
| M5 | 36 | 55 |
| L1 | 55 | 90 |

## 2. Annual Merit Increase

The annual increment cycle is effective 1 July. Merit increases follow the performance rating:

| Rating | Label | Merit increase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Exceptional | 14% to 18% |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | 10% to 13% |
| 3 | Meets expectations | 6% to 9% |
| 2 | Partially meets | 0% to 3% |
| 1 | Does not meet | 0%, with a performance improvement plan (PIP) |

## 3. Variable Pay

Target bonus as a percentage of fixed pay is 8% for E grades, 12% for M1 to M3, 18% for M4 and M5, and 25% for L1.

The actual bonus equals the target bonus multiplied by the company multiplier, which ranges from 0 to 1.5 based on profit after tax (PAT) achievement against plan. The company multiplier for FY2025-26 was 1.1.

Bonuses are paid in August. An employee must be on the company rolls on the payout date to receive the bonus.

## 4. Employee Stock Options

The ESOP pool is 3% of fully diluted shares. Options vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff, at 25% per year.

## 5. Promotions

An employee is eligible for promotion after a minimum of 2 years in the current grade and a rating of 4 or higher in each of the last two cycles. A promotion carries an increase of 15% to 20% on top of the merit increase, capped at the maximum of the new grade's band.

## 6. Pay Equity

The CHRO conducts an annual pay equity audit. A gender pay gap report is presented to the Board by 30 September each year.
